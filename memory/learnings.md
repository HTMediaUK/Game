## Look and feel

- **G-001** (seen 1) Characters use the chunky big-head pixel style from HT's references: blocky head about half the height, solid hair shape, 1x2 eyes, thick brows, small mouth, flat skin with one shadow, heavy dark outline. Never long side-profile faces or big toothy grins (read as "Quagmire" and "creepy"). *(HT's style references and corrections · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-002** (seen 1) Dan is clean-shaven in the game. *("remove the beard and give him a normal jaw" · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-003** (seen 1) Dan's t-shirt says HT in white, not the rainbow logo. *("make his t-shirt say HT" · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-004** (seen 1) Running uses short steps with both feet near the ground (option C), at about 10 frames a second, never tied to distance. *("C for the foot", "moves way too fast" · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-005** (seen 1) The backdrop stays uncluttered: few, evenly spaced turbines, cows and trees, and buildings stand on level ground rather than cutting into the hill. *("way too busy … sit nicely in the hill" · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-006** (seen 1) The game over card is the plain GAME OVER panel, not the clapperboard. *("layout of final card is messy, go back" · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-007** (seen 1) Obstacles must read at a glance: the softbox light didn't, so it became a roadworks barrier. *("softbox light isn't clear" · 20/11/2026)*

## Gameplay

- **G-008** (seen 1) Arrow keys choose the runner on the select screen; after that the game is Space only (Enter just saves the name). *("Select screen can use arrows … then it's just spacebar" · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-009** (seen 1) Difficulty rises with time so no run lasts much more than a minute. *("no one should be playing for more than a minute" · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-010** (seen 1) The crash shout is "YA BUGGER!". *(HT's pick from five North East options · 20/11/2026)*
- **G-011** (seen 1) The game is called HT Dash. *("Change name to HT DASH" · 20/11/2026)*
